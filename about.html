About / The Designer

I help product teams turn complicated, high-stakes workflows into software people can actually operate.

The short version

For eight years I've designed enterprise software in domains where the cost of a confusing interface is real — a banking permission that breaks compliance, a clinical-trial form that delays a study, a logistics dock that backs up an entire network. My work sits at the front of the product, not the end of it: I'm in the room for the discovery and the trade-offs, then I own the system that makes the answer scale. I've led design for platforms at SBI, Roche, Telefónica, and Safexpress, and built the design systems and research practice that let small teams ship without thrashing.

I'm most useful where the problem is genuinely hard and the users aren't like me — warehouse staff, trial coordinators, bank administrators — and the job is to design for how they actually work, not how a clean Figma file wishes they did.
