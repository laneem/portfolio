About Me

The person
behind the work

I'm a Senior UX Designer with 7+ years of hands-on experience across banking, logistics, healthcare, and telecom. I turn complex systems into intuitive, human-centred products — from field portals for billion-dollar enterprises to design systems used by platforms serving millions.

My personal is rooted in research — I believe before I design. I've run discovery sprints, designed for billion-dollar enterprises, and built design-sensitive teams. A good UI is invisible. It just goes all the way to wherever people want to go.

"Design is not just how it looks, it's how it works — for every human, in every context."
