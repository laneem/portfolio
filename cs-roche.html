The Problem

Workflows that were fragmented, silent, and blind

The Rave Support Portal serves as the backbone for clinical trial operations — but the workflows were fragmented. Clinical staff had no single place to manage support, track cases, or access commonly-used forms. Every action required navigating multiple disconnected systems, leading to delays, errors, and frustration.

Four Decisions

Four decisions that changed the experience

Updates and alerts — visible the moment you log in. A persistent notification layer ensuring no clinical update is missed.

Bookmark most-used forms for instant access. Personalised quick-access reduced navigation time dramatically.

Dynamic, field-aware help — right where the user needs it. Contextual guidance reduced support calls significantly.

After one form, you're guided to the next. Sequential form logic eliminated confusion about next steps.

What Enterprise Healthcare Taught Me

Compliance shapes design. Empathy drives it.

Designing for clinical trial staff means designing within strict regulatory constraints. Every interaction had to be auditable, accessible, and error-proof. The lesson: constraints don't kill creativity — they focus it. Prioritise decisions before actions. Focus on user clarity, not interface complexity. Compliance by design, not as an afterthought.
