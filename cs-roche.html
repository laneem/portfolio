Overview

One platform for all Rave clinical trial support

The Rave Support Portal (RSP) serves as the backbone for clinical trial operations at Roche. Clinical trial staff need a single, intelligent place to raise support queries, track cases, access commonly-used forms, and get contextual help — without navigating multiple disconnected systems. The existing platform had 12+ users globally relying on fragmented, unreliable workflows.

The Problem

The workflows were fragmented, silent, and blind

Clinical staff had no single place to manage support, track cases, or access commonly-used forms. Every action required navigating multiple disconnected systems, leading to delays, errors, and frustration. The three core problem areas that emerged from discovery:

No form guidance: Users had no idea what information was needed before starting a form — leading to abandonment mid-way. No status visibility: After submitting a request, users had no way to know what was happening with it. No contextual help: Users had to leave the platform entirely to look up how to complete a specific field or form.

Four Decisions

Four decisions that changed the experience

Decision 1

Updates and alerts — visible the moment you log in

A persistent notification layer ensuring no clinical update is ever missed. Key features: Prioritised alert queue showing newest critical items first. One-click acknowledgement for routine updates. Drill-down to full case detail without leaving the notifications panel. Colour-coded severity system aligned with clinical urgency standards.

Decision 2

Bookmark your most-used forms for instant access

Personalised quick-access reduced navigation time dramatically. Users can bookmark any form or workflow to their personal dashboard. The system surfaces recently-used forms automatically, and learns which forms a user accesses most to suggest shortcuts proactively.

Decision 3

Dynamic, field-aware help — right where the user needs it

Contextual guidance reduced support calls significantly. The RSP now surfaces help content dynamically based on which field or form the user is currently interacting with. No need to leave the platform — relevant guidance, examples, and validation rules appear inline, triggered by the active context.

Decision 4

After one form, you're guided to the next

Sequential form logic eliminated confusion about next steps. After completing any form, the RSP presents a clear summary confirmation and surfaces the logical next action — whether that's a related form, a status check, or a handoff to another team. Users always know what happened and what comes next.

Design Principles

Principles that shaped every decision

Believe before you see: Don't ask users to trust the system. Show them status, progress, and confirmation proactively — before they wonder.

Personalisation as possibility: Every user's workflow is different. The interface adapts to their most common paths — making the familiar faster without confusing the new.

Compliance by design: Regulatory requirements aren't obstacles — they're constraints that sharpen the design. Every interaction is auditable, traceable, and error-resistant by default.

What Enterprise Healthcare Taught Me

Compliance shapes design. Empathy drives it.

Complexity requires design clarity, not design simplicity. Clinical workflows are genuinely complex — simplifying them naively removes necessary information. The challenge is making complexity manageable, not invisible.

Focus on user clarity, not interface aesthetics. In healthcare contexts, a user's ability to find and complete the right form quickly is directly tied to patient outcomes. Beauty matters — but clarity matters more.

Prioritise decisions before actions. Regulatory requirements in clinical trials mean users can't undo many actions. Design must surface the right information before a user commits — not after.
